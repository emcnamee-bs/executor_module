TRADE: Whether natural gas futures price is above a given strike (USD/MMBtu) at 5:00 PM EDT on Oct 30, 2026.
SETTLES ON: Close of the 1-minute candlestick ending at 5:00:00 PM EDT on the specified exchange feed; rounded to 3 decimals; falls back to last published price if data missing.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Weather: cold snaps or heat waves raising heating/cooling demand forecasts, shifts in NOAA/CPC outlooks, early winter storms
- Supply: changes in US natural gas production, drilling rig counts, pipeline outages, LNG export facility disruptions or restarts (e.g. Freeport LNG)
- Storage data: weekly EIA natural gas storage report (injections/withdrawals vs expectations), total storage levels vs 5-year average
- Demand: industrial demand shifts, power-burn demand for electricity generation, LNG export demand from Asia/Europe
- Geopolitics: Russia-Europe gas flows, Middle East conflict affecting LNG shipping, sanctions on energy exports
- Macro/financial: broad commodity selloffs or rallies, dollar strength, crude oil price moves correlating with energy complex, fund positioning/speculative flows
- Scheduled releases: EIA Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report (Thursdays 10:30 AM ET), EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook, weather model updates (GFS/European runs)
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Settlement is a single snapshot price, not a daily average, so intraday volatility near 5:00 PM EDT matters most; the 5:00 PM EDT timing often coincides with end-of-day futures settlement window, which can see thin liquidity and sharp moves; rounding to 3 decimals means tiny price differences can flip resolution near strikes.
IGNORE: Crude oil price moves unrelated to gas fundamentals, unrelated commodity markets (metals, grains), equity market moves, long-term natural gas demand forecasts beyond the settlement date, electricity price news not tied to gas-fired generation, historical price records not relevant to Oct 30 2026 snapshot.
