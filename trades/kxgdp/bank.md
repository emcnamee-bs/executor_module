TRADE: Whether BEA's Advance Estimate of Q3 2026 annualized real GDP growth exceeds a given threshold (e.g., >4.0%, >3.0%, >0.0%).
SETTLES ON: BEA's seasonally adjusted, annualized Advance Estimate for Q3 2026 real GDP growth, published to one decimal; market resolves YES if growth is strictly greater than the stated strike value.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Consumer spending: retail sales reports, consumer confidence surveys, wage growth, credit card spending data, holiday shopping trends
- Business investment: capex surveys, durable goods orders, ISM manufacturing/services PMI, corporate earnings guidance, factory orders
- Government spending: federal/state budget changes, defense spending, infrastructure bill disbursements, government shutdown risk
- Net exports: trade balance data, tariff announcements, dollar strength/weakness, global demand for US goods, supply chain disruptions
- Scheduled data releases: monthly jobs reports, CPI/PCE inflation prints, industrial production, housing starts, GDPNow tracker updates from Atlanta Fed
- Monetary/fiscal policy: Federal Reserve rate decisions, FOMC statements, tax policy changes, stimulus or austerity measures
- Geopolitical/external shocks: oil price spikes, war or sanctions disrupting trade, pandemic resurgence, major supply chain crises
- Weather/disasters: hurricanes, severe storms, droughts affecting agriculture or energy production, natural disasters disrupting output
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Advance Estimate is first of three BEA releases for the quarter (later revised in Second/Third Estimates, but only Advance Estimate counts here), figure is annualized not quarter-over-quarter raw change, release typically occurs about one month after quarter end (late October 2026), data revisions to prior quarters do not affect this market, BEA methodology changes or benchmark revisions could shift historical comparability, government shutdowns can delay the release date and thus market expiration.
IGNORE: Stock market daily volatility, cryptocurrency price swings, individual company earnings unless macro-significant, local/regional economic news without national GDP impact, long-term structural economic debates unrelated to Q3 2026 specifically, GDP data for other countries or other US quarters.
