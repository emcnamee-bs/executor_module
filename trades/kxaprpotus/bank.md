TRADE: KXAPRPOTUS, the weekly ladder of percentage-band markets on the U.S. President's approval rating as shown by the RealClearPolitics polling average
SETTLES ON: the approval number displayed on RealClearPolitics's Trump approval-rating aggregate page (realclearpolling.com/polls/approval/donald-trump/approval-rating) at 11:00 AM ET on the strike date
MOVES THE PRICE:
- New approval polls that feed the RCP average: Rasmussen, Quinnipiac, Economist/YouGov, Morning Consult, Reuters/Ipsos, Gallup, Emerson, Marist releases
- Economic news voters feel: jobs reports, CPI inflation, gas prices, stock market swings, recession talk, tariffs and trade deals
- Major presidential actions: executive orders, vetoes, signed or failed legislation, cabinet firings and nominations
- Scandals and legal matters: indictments, court rulings against the administration, investigations, impeachment moves
- Foreign policy and security: wars, military strikes, terror attacks, summits, hostage or treaty news
- Disasters and crises handled by the White House: hurricanes, pandemics, shutdowns, mass shootings
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: the RCP average is a rolling mean of recent polls so one poll moves it a fraction of a point, it reprices only when RCP adds or drops a poll, the reading is a snapshot at a fixed moment not a weekly mean, approval moves slowly and rarely more than a point in a week
IGNORE: approval ratings of other politicians or foreign leaders, sports polls and rankings, consumer product ratings, celebrity news, local crime with no national political angle
