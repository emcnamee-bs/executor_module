TRADE: Whether a US federal government shutdown (due to lapsed appropriations) is in effect at 10:00 AM ET on Dec 12, 2026.
SETTLES ON: Resolves Yes if OMB/OPM status shows a lapse-driven shutdown (e.g., OMB 'orderly shutdown' directive, OPM status noting 'partial lapse in appropriations') in effect at the specified time; No if funding is in place or OMB directs normal operations despite a technical lapse.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Appropriations status: continuing resolution (CR) passage or failure, omnibus bill progress, deadline extensions, lapse dates
- Congressional action: House/Senate vote counts, whip counts, filibuster threats, reconciliation disputes, leadership negotiations
- White House stance: presidential veto threats, signing statements, OMB guidance, shutdown contingency planning
- Political dynamics: partisan gridlock over riders (border wall, healthcare, debt ceiling), election-year brinkmanship, government funding fights tied to unrelated policy demands
- Market/analyst signals: prediction market odds shifts, pundit/whip-count commentary, historical pattern comparisons to prior shutdowns
- Calendar events: fiscal year deadlines, holiday recesses affecting legislative schedule, prior temporary funding patch expiration dates
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Only a lapse-in-appropriations shutdown counts, not holiday/weather closures; OMB can avert Yes by directing normal operations even after technical lapse; OPM's official operating status page and OMB directives are authoritative sources; partial shutdowns (some agencies funded, others not) still resolve Yes; timing is exact (10:00 AM ET Dec 12, 2026), so late-night or early-morning resolution right before the deadline matters.
IGNORE: State or local government shutdowns, foreign government shutdowns, agency-specific furloughs unrelated to appropriations lapses, debt ceiling default distinct from appropriations lapse, weather/holiday closures, historical past shutdowns unless directly predictive of 2026 timeline, unrelated budget reconciliation bills not tied to the Dec 2026 funding deadline.
