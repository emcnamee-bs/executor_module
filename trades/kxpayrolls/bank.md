TRADE: October 2026 US nonfarm payrolls change vs. threshold (e.g., >90,000)
SETTLES ON: BLS Employment Situation Report for October 2026, specifically the headline month-over-month change in total nonfarm payroll employment (seasonally adjusted).
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Labor demand indicators: ADP private payrolls report, JOLTS job openings, weekly initial jobless claims trends, Challenger layoff announcements
- Hiring/layoff news: major corporate layoffs or hiring sprees, government shutdown/furlough effects, federal workforce reductions
- Sector strength: manufacturing ISM employment index, services PMI employment component, construction/retail/healthcare hiring trends
- Macro conditions: GDP growth signals, consumer spending data, Fed rate decisions affecting hiring, recession fears
- Scheduled releases: prior months' payroll revisions, BLS birth-death model adjustments, benchmark revisions
- Government/policy: shutdown-related data delays, strikes affecting payroll counts, minimum wage or labor policy changes
- Weather/seasonal: hurricanes or major storms disrupting work in survey reference week, unusual seasonal adjustment effects
- Geopolitics/trade: tariffs impacting manufacturing jobs, trade war effects on export sectors, supply chain disruptions
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: BLS uses survey reference week (pay period including 12th of month), initial estimate often revised in following two reports, government shutdowns can delay or distort data collection, strikes during reference week reduce reported payrolls temporarily, birth-death model adds estimated unmeasured business formation/closure jobs.
IGNORE: unemployment rate changes (separate household survey metric), average hourly earnings/wage growth, labor force participation rate, stock market reactions to jobs data itself.
