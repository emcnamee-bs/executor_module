TRADE: Whether Donald Trump attends a specific upcoming UFC event.
SETTLES ON: Resolves YES if any Source Agency (news outlet, UFC/TKO statement, or Trump's own social media) reports him present at the event, even briefly; otherwise resolves NO.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Trump's public schedule: White House itinerary releases, campaign or official travel plans, reports of conflicting events same day
- Trump's past attendance pattern: history of attending high-profile UFC fights (e.g., with Dana White), frequency of recent sightings at sporting events
- Dana White/UFC statements: UFC promotional announcements naming VIP attendees, ticket or seating arrangements mentioned publicly
- Security/logistics news: Secret Service advisories, venue security plans, reports of VIP box reservations
- Social media activity: Trump or his team posting about plans to attend, teaser posts from UFC account tagging Trump
- Political/legal distractions: court dates, legislative votes, international trips, health issues that could prevent travel
- Weather/travel disruptions: flight cancellations, severe weather near event city affecting travel plans
- Event prominence: whether the UFC card feature's a marquee fighter or title bout, increasing likelihood of celebrity attendance
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Any credible report of presence counts even if only a brief appearance or walk-through, self-reported social media posts (e.g., a selfie or tweet from the venue) are sufficient for YES, denials or lack of any reporting by event end result in NO, the specific UFC event number changes each cycle but resolution logic is identical, attendance must be at the exact named event not a different UFC card.
IGNORE: General UFC fight results or fighter performance, other celebrities' attendance unrelated to Trump, Trump's opinions or comments about UFC/MMA unrelated to physical attendance, ticket sales or pay-per-view numbers for the event, unrelated political news about Trump not tied to this specific event's date, historical UFC event attendance statistics.
