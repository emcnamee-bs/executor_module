TRADE: Will COMEX/spot gold price close above a given USD/t.oz strike at 5:00 PM EDT on Oct 30, 2026 (1-minute candle).
SETTLES ON: The close of the 1-minute gold candlestick timestamped 5:00 PM EDT Oct 30 2026 (reflecting the 4:59:00-4:59:59 PM trading window); if no data, most recent prior published price is used.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Monetary policy: Fed rate decisions, FOMC minutes, Fed chair speeches, rate-cut/hike odds shifts, other central bank gold buying or policy moves (ECB, PBOC, BOJ)
- Macro data releases: US CPI, PPI, jobs report/NFP, GDP, retail sales, PCE inflation, ISM manufacturing data
- US dollar moves: DXY index strength/weakness, major currency pair swings (EUR/USD, USD/JPY)
- Safe-haven demand/geopolitics: war escalation, Middle East conflict, Russia-Ukraine news, China-Taiwan tensions, terrorist attacks, sanctions
- Treasury yields and real rates: 10-year yield moves, TIPS yields, bond market selloffs or rallies
- Market risk sentiment: stock market crashes/rallies, banking crisis, credit events, flight-to-safety or risk-on flows
- Physical/ETF demand and supply: central bank gold reserve purchases, SPDR Gold Trust (GLD) flows, mine supply disruptions, Indian/Chinese jewelry demand, Diwali/wedding season buying
- Scheduled data/events calendar: upcoming FOMC meeting dates, CPI release dates, options expiry, month-end rebalancing flows
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Price is the official COMEX/spot gold 1-minute candle close exactly at 5:00 PM EDT, not an average or daily close; late-day thin liquidity can cause brief spikes/dips that lock in at settlement; weekend/holiday proximity (Oct 30 2026 is a Friday) can cause pre-weekend position squaring; if feed is down, last published tick stands in, so a stale price could diverge from true market level.
IGNORE: silver, platinum, or palladium price moves, oil and other commodity price news unrelated to gold, general stock market commentary without gold-specific linkage, cryptocurrency price moves, long-term gold price forecasts/analyst targets not tied to Oct 30 2026, mining company earnings unrelated to gold spot pricing.
