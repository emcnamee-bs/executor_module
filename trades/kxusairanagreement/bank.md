TRADE: Whether the US and Iran agree to a new formal nuclear deal with sanctions relief before a given deadline (nested deadlines through Jan 2029).
SETTLES ON: Kalshi rules: the US has agreed to, signed, or accepted a written agreement with Iran (multilateral is OK) that imposes verifiable nuclear restrictions AND relieves at least one US sanction; both governments officially agreeing to the terms suffices even before signing.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Diplomatic talks: announced negotiation rounds, Oman/Qatar/Swiss mediation, Witkoff or Araghchi meetings, framework or interim deal announcements, talks collapsing or being suspended
- Military and geopolitics: US or Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear sites, Iranian retaliation, Gulf shipping attacks, Houthi or Hezbollah escalation, ceasefires
- Nuclear program developments: IAEA reports, enrichment levels, 60% stockpile, inspector access, NPT withdrawal threats, UN snapback sanctions
- Sanctions and economy: OFAC designations, oil export waivers, frozen asset releases, rial collapse, Iranian oil exports to China
- US politics: Trump statements and ultimatums, Congressional review demands, Republican hawk opposition, sanctions bills
- Iranian politics: Khamenei statements, President Pezeshkian, hardline parliament, IRGC positions, leadership succession turmoil
- Allies and third parties: Israeli lobbying, E3 positions, Russia and China roles, Saudi involvement
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: only a formal written instrument counts, vague verbal understandings are insufficient, restrictions must be verifiable (inspections or monitoring), sanctions relief must be included, ceasefires or prisoner swaps alone do not count, a joint announcement of agreed terms is enough, later deadlines are supersets of earlier ones
IGNORE: Iran sports or cultural news, Iran human rights cases with no talks link, North Korea nuclear talks, Russia-Ukraine deals, domestic Iranian protests with no diplomatic effect, generic oil price commentary
