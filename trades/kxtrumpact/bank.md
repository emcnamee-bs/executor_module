TRADE: Weekly count of Trump presidential actions (EOs, proclamations, memoranda, nominations/appointments) published on whitehouse.gov's Presidential Actions page, Oct 4-10, 2026.
SETTLES ON: Count of items with publication dates in the window, as visibly listed on whitehouse.gov Presidential Actions tab at 10:00 AM ET Oct 11, 2026.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Policy agenda pace: reports of planned executive orders, proclamations, or memoranda on immigration, trade, energy, healthcare
- Nominations/appointments: Senate confirmation pushes, new agency head picks, judicial nominations batches
- Crisis/urgent response: natural disasters, national emergencies, government shutdown, military action prompting rapid proclamations
- Legislative/ceremonial calendar: bill signings, National Days/weeks proclamations, routine recurring designations
- Travel/schedule: Trump's domestic/foreign travel reducing signing activity, holidays or recess slowing output
- Staff/White House counsel bottlenecks: reports of backlog, delayed publication, document review slowdowns
- Tariff/trade actions: new tariff proclamations, trade deal implementation orders
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Only publication date counts, not signing date; backdated items don't count; items must appear specifically on Presidential Actions page, not Briefings & Statements; late uploads after 10 AM cutoff don't count even if signed earlier; nominations/appointments count same as EOs; any item counts regardless of legal effect or significance.
IGNORE: Congressional legislation not signed by Trump, state governors' executive orders, agency rulemaking/regulations not from president, Trump's tweets/speeches/press statements without formal action, polling on Trump approval, court rulings blocking prior orders, cabinet meeting discussions without resulting documents.
