TRADE: Whether September 2026 US CPI (month-over-month, seasonally adjusted, single-decimal) rises above a specified threshold.
SETTLES ON: BLS-published single-decimal September 2026 CPI m/m % change released October 14, 2026 (delayed if shutdown disrupts data).
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Energy prices: gasoline price spikes/drops, OPEC+ production cuts or increases, crude oil price swings, natural gas price changes, refinery outages
- Food/commodity prices: grocery price inflation, agricultural supply shocks, drought or crop failures, meat/egg price surges, food distribution disruptions
- Shelter/housing costs: rent index trends, owners' equivalent rent changes, home price appreciation, housing supply constraints
- Monetary/fiscal policy: Federal Reserve rate decisions, QE/QT changes, fiscal stimulus packages, tax policy changes, government spending shifts
- Supply chain and trade: tariff announcements, import/export disruptions, shipping cost spikes, semiconductor shortages, port congestion, supply chain bottlenecks
- Labor market: wage growth reports, labor strikes, minimum wage increases, employment cost index changes
- Geopolitical events: war-related commodity disruptions, sanctions affecting energy/commodities, currency crises affecting import prices
- Seasonal/weather: hurricanes affecting energy infrastructure, extreme weather disrupting food supply, seasonal adjustment anomalies
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: CPI figure is seasonally adjusted so raw unadjusted price moves may not translate directly, single-decimal rounding means small revisions can flip resolution near threshold boundaries, BLS methodology includes substitution and hedonic adjustments that dampen some price signals, data can be delayed by government shutdowns extending the resolution date up to six months, core vs headline CPI differ so energy/food volatility affects headline more than core.
IGNORE: core CPI or PCE inflation measures (different index), prior months' CPI prints or year-over-year CPI figures, PPI (producer price index) releases, stock market reactions to CPI, unrelated inflation expectations surveys, CPI figures for other countries.
