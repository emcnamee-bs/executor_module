TRADE: Whether the WTI crude oil front-month futures daily settlement price on a given upcoming date finishes above a stated USD/bbl strike.
SETTLES ON: Daily settlement price of the NYMEX WTI futures contract for the nearest listed month, rolling to the next month 2 business days before the current contract's last trading day; rounded to 2 decimals; last available data used if none is published.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Supply policy: OPEC+ output decisions, Saudi or Russian export quotas, voluntary cuts or unwinding of cuts, compliance and quota disputes
- Geopolitics and sanctions: Middle East conflict, Strait of Hormuz or Red Sea shipping threats, Russia-Ukraine refinery or pipeline strikes, sanctions on Iran, Russia or Venezuela, tanker seizures
- US inventories and production: EIA weekly crude stocks, Cushing storage levels, API reports, rig counts, shale output, refinery utilization, SPR releases or refills
- Demand and macro: China import and industrial data, US and global GDP, jobs and inflation prints, Fed rate decisions, recession fears, jet fuel and gasoline demand
- Dollar and financial flows: US dollar strength, risk-on or risk-off equity moves, hedge fund positioning, futures curve backwardation or contango, options expiry
- Weather and disruptions: Gulf of Mexico hurricanes, refinery outages, pipeline shutdowns, Libya or Nigeria outages, Canadian wildfires, winter freezes
- Scheduled releases: EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report, OPEC and IEA monthly reports, EIA STEO, Baker Hughes rig count, FOMC meetings, US-China trade talks
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Only the daily settlement on the target date counts, not intraday highs or lows; settlement is set by the exchange in a window around the 2:30pm ET close; the front month rolls 2 business days before expiry, so the reference contract can change near expiry and the calendar spread then matters; news after the settlement time affects only the next day; weekend or holiday news is priced at the next open; the strike is a threshold, so moves matter relative to the current price and the gap to the strike; Brent moves usually transmit to WTI.
IGNORE: retail gasoline pump prices alone, natural gas or coal prices, oil company earnings or stock picks, electric vehicle sales news, Brent-only regional spreads with no crude effect, climate policy speeches with no supply impact, energy company mergers
