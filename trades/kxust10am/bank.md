TRADE: Whether the 10-Year U.S. Treasury par yield closes above 5.63% on Oct 30, 2026.
SETTLES ON: U.S. Treasury par yield curve data release for Oct 30, 2026, as published by the U.S. Treasury Department (Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates); market expires at 7PM ET following that release or one week after Oct 30, 2026, whichever is sooner.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- Fed policy: FOMC rate decisions, dot plot changes, Fed Chair speeches on rate path, QT/QE balance sheet announcements, hawkish or dovish Fed minutes
- Inflation data: CPI, PCE, core PCE releases, inflation expectations surveys (University of Michigan, NY Fed), wage growth data
- Economic growth signals: GDP reports, jobs reports (nonfarm payrolls), ISM manufacturing/services PMI, retail sales, unemployment rate
- Treasury supply/issuance: Treasury refunding announcements, auction sizes for 10Y notes, bid-to-cover ratios, deficit/debt ceiling news, fiscal policy changes
- Geopolitical/safety-flight events: wars, sanctions, banking crises, flight-to-quality moves, foreign central bank reserve policy (China, Japan buying/selling Treasuries)
- Global rate differentials: ECB/BOJ/BOE policy moves, foreign bond yield shifts, currency volatility affecting capital flows into US debt
- Credit rating actions: Moody's/S&P/Fitch rating changes or outlook revisions on US sovereign debt
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Uses official Treasury par yield curve, not secondary market intraday quotes, so there can be slight timing lag versus real-time bond market pricing, data is published once daily after market close around 3:30-4:30 PM ET, par yields are interpolated from actual Treasury trading activity and can differ slightly from benchmark 10Y note yields quoted elsewhere, no yield is published on holidays or weekends so the relevant reference is the most recent prior trading day's data if Oct 30, 2026 falls on a non-trading day.
IGNORE: stock market daily moves unrelated to rates, corporate bond spreads, mortgage rate specifics unless tied to Treasury yields, short-term T-bill or 2Y yield moves without 10Y context, cryptocurrency price action, unrelated commodity price swings.
