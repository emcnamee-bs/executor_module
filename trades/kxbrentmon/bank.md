TRADE: Level of Brent crude oil price (USD/Bbl) at a specific moment (5:00 PM EDT, October 30, 2026).
SETTLES ON: The close price of the 1-minute candlestick for Brent crude at 5:00:00 PM EDT on October 30, 2026, as published by the exchange/data source; if unavailable, the most recent prior published price is used.
MOVES THE PRICE:
- OPEC+ supply policy: production quota decisions, compliance/overproduction by members, surprise output cuts or increases, Saudi/Russia unilateral moves
- Geopolitical supply risk: Middle East conflict (Iran, Israel, Gulf states), Strait of Hormuz disruption threats, Russia sanctions or export bans, attacks on tankers/pipelines/refineries, Venezuela or Libya outages
- US/global demand signals: China manufacturing PMI, US GDP or recession fears, global growth forecasts from IEA/EIA/OPEC monthly reports, jet fuel and gasoline demand trends
- Scheduled data releases: weekly EIA crude inventory report (Wednesdays), API inventory estimate (Tuesdays), EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook, IEA Oil Market Report, OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report, Baker Hughes rig count
- Macro/financial drivers: US dollar strength/weakness, Fed interest rate decisions, inflation data, equity market risk sentiment, bond yields
- Weather and seasonal: hurricanes threatening Gulf of Mexico production/refineries, extreme cold affecting demand or production (freeze-offs), winter heating demand
- Refinery and infrastructure: refinery outages or restarts, pipeline disruptions, strategic petroleum reserve releases or purchases
SETTLEMENT-SENSITIVE FACTS: Uses a single 1-minute candlestick close, so settlement reflects instantaneous price not daily average; thinly traded after-hours periods can show unusual volatility or stale quotes; if no trade prints at that exact minute, prior published data carries forward; Brent (not WTI) is the benchmark, so North Sea/European supply factors matter more than US-only factors; price is in USD so FX moves affecting dollar strength matter.
IGNORE: WTI-specific news without Brent linkage, US gasoline retail pump prices (downstream, not Brent), unrelated equity market moves, natural gas price news, long-term multi-year oil demand forecasts unrelated to near-term price, electric vehicle adoption trends, corporate earnings of individual oil companies.
